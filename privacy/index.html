ExpenseMY Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

ExpenseMY is a personal expense tracker for Android, made by Chai Chun. It turns payment alerts from your banking and e-wallet apps into spending records, and it works entirely on your phone. This page explains what the app reads, what it keeps, and what (very little) ever leaves your phone.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your records stay on your phone, encrypted. The developer never receives your notifications, transactions or name.
  • No analytics and no tracking. Your data is never sold or shared.
  • You are in control. You choose which apps are read, and you can turn it off or delete everything at any time.

1. What ExpenseMY reads

  • Notifications from the apps you choose. With Android's Notification access, which you grant and can revoke at any time, ExpenseMY reads notifications from the banking and e-wallet apps you select in the app: currently MAE, CIMB OCTO, Touch 'n Go eWallet, Grab, Boost and Atome. ShopeePay and Google Wallet can also be read, but only if you turn them on; they are off by default. These notifications can contain amounts, merchant names, dates and partial account details.
  • Notifications from all other apps are ignored. They are not stored or processed.
  • What you enter yourself, such as manual transactions, categories, notes, splits and rules. You can also enter your legal or account name. It is optional and used only to recognise transfers to your own account.

ExpenseMY is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any bank or e-wallet named on this page. It does not connect to your bank and never sees your banking password.

2. Why it reads them

Only to record your spending automatically, show you spending summaries, and put unclear alerts in a Review list for you to check. It is not used for any other purpose.

3. Where your data is kept and how it is protected

  • Your data is stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database (SQLCipher). Its key is protected by the Android Keystore.
  • The data is excluded from Android and Google cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer, so it is not copied anywhere automatically.
  • The app is locked with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android handles this. ExpenseMY never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Notification log: by default ExpenseMY keeps the original text of captured notifications from your chosen apps, up to the 5,000 most recent, so you can see what happened to each one. In Settings → Privacy & Security you can switch this to metadata only (no text) or off. You can also clear the log at any time.
  • Alerts ExpenseMY shows, such as "Automatically recorded", hide amounts and merchants on the lock screen.

4. What leaves your phone

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

  • ExpenseMY on Google Play does not have Android's internet permission and makes no network connections.
  • Early test copies installed outside Google Play may check GitHub for app updates. These requests contain no data from the app. As with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address and basic device information; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Files leave the app only when you choose to export them:

  • Encrypted backup. It contains your transactions and rules, but not pending reviews, the notification log or app settings. It is protected by a password you set and saved where you choose.
  • Diagnostics export. This is a plain-text file and is not encrypted. It may include notification text and transactions. It is created only when you tap Export and saved where you choose. Share it only with someone you trust, for example the developer when reporting a problem.

If you save or send these files to another service, such as Google Drive, email or WhatsApp, that service's own privacy policy applies.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. ExpenseMY contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting code.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn off Notification access for ExpenseMY in Android settings at any time.
  • Choose which apps are read in Settings → Listening apps.
  • Delete recorded transactions in Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear recorded transactions. Clear the notification log from Notification Logs.
  • Uninstalling ExpenseMY deletes all of its data from your phone, including the encryption key. It cannot be recovered unless you saved an encrypted backup.

Because the developer holds none of your app data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. Everything is on your phone and under your control.

7. If you contact us or join testing

If you email or message us, we receive your contact details and whatever you send. If you join a Google Play test, we see the email address you were added with. We use this only to reply, run the test and fix problems, and delete it when it is no longer needed. Google Play's handling of your account is covered by Google's privacy policy.

8. Children

ExpenseMY is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also appear in the app's release notes.
