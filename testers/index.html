ExpenseMY Tester Guide

Thank you for helping test ExpenseMY before it launches on Google Play.

ExpenseMY records your spending automatically from the payment alerts your banking and e-wallet apps already send you. Everything stays encrypted on your phone. This page explains how to join, set it up in a few minutes, and tell us when something goes wrong.

The one thing we need most: please stay in the test and keep ExpenseMY installed for at least 14 days. Google only lets the app launch after enough testers have stayed in the test for 14 days in a row.

1. Before you start

  • An Android phone with Android 10 or later.
  • A screen lock (PIN, pattern or password). ExpenseMY uses it to lock the app.
  • At least one of these apps, with payment notifications turned on: MAE, CIMB OCTO, Touch 'n Go eWallet, Grab, Boost or Atome. Other banks are not supported yet. Without one of these apps you can still look around with demo data (see section 5).
  • The Google account (Gmail) you use for the Play Store. Send it to Chai Chun so it can be added to the tester list.

2. Join the test and install

  1. Open the test link Chai Chun sends you, while signed in to the same Google account.
  2. Tap Become a tester.
  3. Tap the Google Play link on that page and install ExpenseMY. It can take a few minutes after joining before the app appears.
  4. Updates arrive through Google Play like any other app.

If you previously installed ExpenseMY from a file (APK), tell Chai Chun before installing from Google Play.

3. First-time setup (about 3 minutes)

After you unlock ExpenseMY the first time, a Setup checklist opens. Tap Continue setup. It takes you to each Android setting in turn and checks it again when you come back.

  1. Allow notification access. ExpenseMY explains what it reads, and you tap Agree. In Android settings, switch ExpenseMY on. It only reads the apps you choose in the next step.
  2. Choose your bank & wallet apps. The supported apps already on your phone are ticked for you. Untick any you don't want recorded.
  3. Turn on recording alerts. Allow notifications so ExpenseMY can tell you when it recorded something. You can send yourself a test alert.
  4. Let ExpenseMY run in the background. Set its battery setting to "Unrestricted" or "Don't optimize". Otherwise Android may stop it and payments will be missed.
  5. Allow auto-start (Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, OPPO, realme, OnePlus, vivo, Huawei and Honor only). Turn on auto-start for ExpenseMY on the page that opens, then come back and confirm.
  6. Lock it in Recent apps (Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO only). Open Recent apps, long-press ExpenseMY and choose the lock, so "Clear all" doesn't close it.
  7. Your legal name (optional). This is used only to recognise transfers to your own account. You can skip it.

Tap Finish setup. You should see Listening. You can reopen Setup at any time from the top of Settings.

4. Everyday use

  • Pay as usual. A "Recorded" alert appears with buttons to pick a category straight away.
  • Alerts ExpenseMY isn't sure about go to the Review tab. Check them now and then.
  • Open a transaction and tap Split to count only your share of a shared bill.
  • Add cash spending yourself with the Add button on the Transactions tab.

5. No supported app? Try demo data

Open Settings → Try demo data to explore ExpenseMY with fictional records. A banner shows while you are in demo mode, and Exit demo takes you back. Demo data never mixes with your real records.

6. Founding tester code

Thank you for testing early. As a founding tester you keep ExpenseMY Full for life, free: unlimited automatic recording and the spending calendar. Chai Chun will send you a one-time code. While the test runs, everything in the app is already open to you; the code makes sure it stays that way after launch, when the free version will record up to 5 payments automatically per day and have no calendar.

How to redeem your code
  1. Update ExpenseMY from Google Play to the latest test version first.
  2. In ExpenseMY open Settings → ExpenseMY Full and tap Redeem code. It opens Google Play's redeem page. (Or open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code.)
  3. Use the same Google account you test with, type the code and tap Redeem. You are not charged.
  4. Go back to ExpenseMY. It asks Google Play when it opens, so if Settings still shows the old text, close the app and open it again.

When it worked, Settings → ExpenseMY Full shows Full · unlimited automatic recording and spending calendar and Thank you, founding tester.

  • The code belongs to your Google account, so Full stays after a new phone or a reinstall, as long as you sign in to the same account in Google Play.
  • Each code works once and expires on the date Chai Chun tells you, so redeem it soon. Please don't post it in a group chat.
  • No Google Play on your phone, or the redeem page won't open? Tell Chai Chun. Everything else in the app keeps working.

7. Reporting a problem

Message Chai Chun directly or email loichaichun@gmail.com. Please include:

  • your phone brand and model;
  • what you expected and what happened instead;
  • roughly when it happened, and which app the payment was from;
  • a screenshot if it helps. Hide amounts or names you'd rather not share.
About "Export diagnostics": Chai Chun may ask you for this file to fix a missed payment. It is plain text and can contain your notification text and transactions. Send it only directly to Chai Chun, and never post it in a group chat.

8. Good to know

  • Your records stay on your phone. Uninstalling ExpenseMY deletes them, so use Settings → Backup & Restore to save an encrypted backup first if you want to keep them.
  • Battery and auto-start settings look different on every phone brand. If payments stop being recorded, reopen Setup from Settings and check each item again.
  • Read the privacy policy.
